# Nyrex salon pilot runbook

Use this with one salon at a time. The example salon and all details in the public demo are fictional.

## Before agreeing to setup

- Confirm owner/manager name, salon name, area, and the person who will review handoffs.
- Check the WhatsApp number against the current Nyrex setup. Do not ask the salon to move, disconnect, or migrate a number before explaining options and impact.
- Show the [30-second salon demo](https://nyrex.pages.dev/salon-demo.html).
- State in writing: Nyrex is free for the 14-day pilot; WhatsApp and provider charges may apply; confirm the salon's actual charges before setup.
- Get the owner-approved service list, prices they want shared, opening hours, address, booking process, and topics the assistant must hand off.
- Get written approval to use the number and to review the conversations for the pilot. Tell the owner who can see the data, where handoff alerts go, and how to request deletion.
- Agree who confirms bookings and the hours in which that person can respond. Nyrex only records a request; it must not promise an appointment.

## Configure and test before launch

Test each of these with the owner and save the result:

1. Ask about a service the salon offers: answer must match its approved service list.
2. Ask for a price the salon approved for sharing: answer must match exactly.
3. Ask for a price the salon has not approved: assistant must say staff will follow up.
4. Ask whether it is open on a closed day: answer must match the approved hours.
5. Request a booking: assistant collects service, preferred date/time, and customer name, then says staff will confirm.
6. Ask for a human: assistant hands off with the customer message and does not loop.

Do not launch until all six pass. If one fails, fix it and repeat that case.

## Daily review during the 14 days

Review every conversation for the first three days. After that, review all handoffs and a sample of routine replies each day. For each conversation, record one outcome:

- **Correct:** response matches owner-approved facts and follows the salon's rules.
- **Handoff:** assistant collects the request and gives the owner/staff a clear next action.
- **Needs correction:** inaccurate, incomplete, or overpromises; correct the source/rule before continuing.

If a wrong price, hour, or appointment promise appears, pause that answer path, tell the owner, correct it, and rerun the relevant test before resuming.

## Day 14 decision

Review the scorecard with the owner. Show example conversations only with their permission and redact customer phone numbers. Ask:

1. Which replies saved the team time?
2. Which replies should be changed or handed to staff?
3. Were all booking requests seen and confirmed by staff?
4. Should the salon continue, revise the setup, or stop?

Send the agreed plan and any applicable WhatsApp/provider fees before charging anything.

## Short demo / pilot call script

“First, I’ll check whether your WhatsApp number fits our current setup. You do not need to move or disconnect it to have this conversation. If it is compatible, I’ll show you a 30-second sample using salon-approved hours and services. The assistant can answer routine questions and collect booking requests; your staff still confirms appointment times. The Nyrex service is free during the 14-day pilot. WhatsApp or provider fees may apply, and I’ll confirm your actual costs before setup. If the setup and costs work for you, we’ll test six cases together before going live.”

## Suggested 30-second voiceover

- **0–4s:** “Salon owners are busy with clients, but WhatsApp questions keep coming.”
- **4–11s:** “Nyrex answers routine questions from the salon’s approved services and hours.”
- **11–18s:** “When someone asks for a booking, it collects the request and sends it to the team.”
- **18–24s:** “Staff confirms the time. Nyrex doesn’t promise a slot.”
- **24–30s:** “Try it with a free 14-day pilot. Check your number and any provider fees before setup.”
