Aapke customers ko WhatsApp par turant jawab.

Nyrex aapke business ke WhatsApp messages ka jawab deta hai. Raat ko bhi, aur jab aap kisi customer ke saath busy ho tab bhi. Aapka business kisi bhi type ka ho, chalta hai.

Ye page Hinglish mein hai. Baaki site English mein hai.

Kaise kaam karta hai.

  1. Aap basics batate ho

    Timing, services, booking kaise hoti hai, aur assistant ko kya kabhi promise nahi karna.

  2. Hum aapka number jodte hain

    Aapke business ke WhatsApp number par assistant lag jata hai, WhatsApp ke business platform se.

  3. Jawab assistant deta hai, control aapke paas

    Customer ko turant reply milta hai. Jo baat kisi insaan ko sambhalni ho, woh alert ke saath aapko mil jati hai.

Free pilot.

14 din free. Koi contract nahi. Jab chahein band kar sakte hain.

Pehle hum aapke business ki details par ek 30-second demo dikhate hain, phir aapke saath setup karte hain.

Pilot ke baad agar aap continue karna chahein, to monthly price pehle hi tay hoga. Usse pehle koi charge nahi.

Customer jis language mein likhe, assistant usi mein jawab deta hai. Pilot mein hum wahi languages test karte hain jo aapke customers use karte hain.

Hume aapse kya chahiye.

Pandrah minute ka time. Ek WhatsApp message ya chhota form kaafi hai.

  • Business ka naam, aap kya karte ho, aur kahan ho
  • Timing aur chhutti ke din
  • Services, aur woh prices jo assistant bata sakta hai
  • Booking kaise hoti hai, aur confirm kaun karta hai
  • Ek WhatsApp number (hum isme help karte hain)
  • Alert ke liye email address

Aapke control mein.

  • Assistant sirf wahi batata hai jo aapne diya hai. Pata na ho to woh guess nahi karta, aapko alert bhejta hai.
  • Booking aap ya aapki team confirm karti hai.
  • Customer chahe to insaan se baat maang sakta hai.
  • Aap khud bhi kabhi bhi reply kar sakte ho.

Data ke baare mein poori jaankari English mein hai: Customers ke messages kahan jaate hain, privacy policy aur pilot agreement.
