What the assistant handles

  • Opening hours

    Answered from the hours you give us, including the days you're closed.

  • Services

    Described the way you describe them, in your tone.

  • Prices

    Shared only if you give them to us. Otherwise it says your team will confirm.

  • Booking requests

    Collects name, service, day and time. Your team confirms the slot.

  • Languages

    Replies in the language the customer writes in. We test the ones your customers use.

  • Remembers the chat

    Doesn't ask again for a name or time the customer already gave.

  • Complaints

    Apologises, says a person will be in touch, and flags you right away.

  • Hand-offs

    Anything it can't answer goes to you by email, with the customer's name, number and message.

Live in three steps.

  1. You tell us the basics

    Hours, services, how booking works, and what the assistant should never promise. A short form or a WhatsApp chat is enough.

  2. We check the number first

    Our current setup needs a number not already registered in the regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app. We explain the options and any impact before you decide.

  3. It answers, you stay in control

    Customers get instant replies in their own language. Anything that needs you is flagged right away.
