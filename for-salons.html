WhatsApp automation pilot for Vasai salons

Nyrex is starting with local salons that receive service and appointment questions on WhatsApp. The pilot includes a number-fit check, a review of the salon’s approved information, and a test of replies before anything goes live. There is no Nyrex subscription charge during the 14-day pilot; WhatsApp or provider charges may apply and are confirmed first.

What a salon WhatsApp assistant should handle

Customers often ask about hair colour, facials, bridal makeup, service prices, opening hours and appointment times. Nyrex uses the details your salon approves to answer those routine questions, including after hours.

If you are comparing a salon chatbot or WhatsApp booking assistant, check whether it confirms a real slot or only collects a request. Nyrex does not read a live salon calendar or promise availability: it records the customer’s preferred service and time, then your staff confirms the appointment.

Check WhatsApp number fit before setup

Nyrex’s current setup needs a number not already registered in the regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app. Ask us to check your number first; do not move or disconnect the number your clients use. The Nyrex service is free for 14 days. WhatsApp or provider charges may apply, and we confirm them before setup.
