Delete My Data

Last updated: 8 November 2026

You can ask us to delete what we hold about you at any time. There is no form to fight with: send us an email and we deal with it.

Email a deletion request

What to include

  • If you messaged a business that uses Nyrex: the phone number you messaged from, and the business if you know it. This lets us find the right conversation records.
  • If you sent a demo request: the business name and the contact number you gave.
  • If you are a business that tried Nyrex: your business name. We can delete your setup information and your customers' conversation records.

What happens next

  • We may ask a question to be sure the request comes from you, such as asking you to write from, or message us on, the number involved. This protects your data from anyone else.
  • We delete the records we hold and tell you when it is done. Copies kept by the services we use, such as WhatsApp, are handled under their own terms.
  • If a law requires us to keep something, for example a payment record, we tell you what and why.

Other things you can ask

You can also ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Use the same email. If you are not happy with our answer, write to us again with "Grievance" in the subject and we will look at it.

More detail is on the privacy page and where messages go.
