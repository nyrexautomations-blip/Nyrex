Refund Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Short version: you pay nothing during the 14-day pilot, you pay month by month after that, and you can stop whenever you like.

The free pilot

The first 14 days are free. We do not ask for a card or a payment, so there is nothing to refund.

Monthly plans

  • Plans are paid monthly by UPI. There is no contract and no setup fee.
  • The plan and price are confirmed with you in writing before the first payment.
  • You can stop at any time. We stop charging from the next month. A month you have already paid for is not charged again and is used until it ends.

If you were charged by mistake

If you were charged for something you did not agree to, or charged twice, email nyrexautomations@gmail.com with the date and the UPI reference. We will check it and reply. If the charge was our error we refund it to the same UPI account.

If the service did not work

If the assistant was down or giving wrong answers because of a problem on our side, tell us. We fix it first, and we will discuss a fair credit or refund for the affected time.

Other fees

WhatsApp or provider fees are paid to those providers and are separate from Nyrex. We cannot refund them, but we tell you what applies to your setup before you decide. See pricing.

The terms of service and the pilot agreement cover the rest.
