Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Nyrex builds an assistant that answers WhatsApp messages for local businesses. This page explains what information is handled when you message a business that uses Nyrex, or message our own demo number, and what we do with it. If anything is unclear, email us at nyrexautomations@gmail.com.

Who we are

Nyrex is a small business that sets up automated WhatsApp replies for other businesses. When you message a business that uses Nyrex, that business decides what the assistant should say, and Nyrex runs the assistant for them. When you message Nyrex's own demo number, Nyrex is the business you are talking to.

What information we handle

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the profile name shown on your WhatsApp account.
  • The messages you send and the replies the assistant sends back, with the date and time.
  • Any details you choose to put in a message, for example your name, the service you want, or a preferred day and time.

Please do not send sensitive information such as payment card numbers, passwords or medical details to the assistant. It does not need them.

How we use it

  • To read your message and write a reply.
  • To remember the conversation, so you do not have to repeat yourself.
  • To pass your request to the business's team when the assistant cannot settle it, such as a booking to confirm, a complaint, or a question it has no answer for. In that case an email alert with your name, phone number and message is sent to the business or to Nyrex.
  • To keep a record of conversations, so we can check what was said, fix problems and improve the assistant.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

To make the assistant work, messages pass through other services that act for us:

  • WhatsApp and Meta, which deliver the messages. Their own privacy policy applies to your use of WhatsApp.
  • An automation platform (Make), which runs the steps behind each reply and stores the conversation record.
  • An AI service (Google Gemini), which writes the reply text. It receives the recent conversation, your message and your WhatsApp profile name.
  • Email (Gmail), which sends the hand-off alerts described above.

Each of these services handles data under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

You are talking to an automated assistant

Replies are written by AI. The assistant will say so if you ask. It is told to use only the information the business has given it, but it can still make mistakes, so a person at the business confirms anything important, such as a booking.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation records only as long as they are needed to run the assistant for the business and to fix problems. Records are not deleted automatically yet, so the best way to have yours removed is to ask us (see below).

Your choices

  • Ask what we hold about your number and conversations.
  • Ask us to delete your conversation records. Email us from the details below with the phone number you messaged from, and we will remove them.
  • Stop messaging at any time. If you want a person instead of the assistant, say so in the chat.

This website

This website does not use accounts, tracking cookies or analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when the page loads. If you email or message us, we use the details you send to reply to you.

Children

Nyrex is meant for business use and is not aimed at children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
