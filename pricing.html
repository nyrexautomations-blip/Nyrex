How paying works.

Nothing during the pilot

The first 14 days are free. We only talk about a plan once you have seen it work.

Pay by UPI, monthly

If you continue, we send the UPI details on WhatsApp. You pay once a month and can stop any time.

Agreed in writing first

The plan and price are confirmed with you before anything is charged. See the pilot agreement.

Good to know.

  • Founding businesses. The first businesses to join keep their price for as long as they stay with us.
  • WhatsApp's own charges. Meta, which runs WhatsApp's business platform, has its own message pricing. In most cases replies to customers who message you first cost little or nothing. We show you the current rules before you decide.
  • What is not included. Nyrex does not confirm bookings or agree custom quotes. Your team does, and the assistant passes those to you.
  • Your data stays yours. If you leave, we delete it. Read where messages go.
