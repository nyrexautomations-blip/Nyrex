Questions you might have.

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, message us and we'll add it.

The basics

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. They message your WhatsApp number as usual.

How fast does it reply?

Within seconds, at any hour.

Which languages does it reply in?

The language the customer writes in. How well it does depends on the language, so during the pilot we test the ones your customers actually use.

What if someone sends a voice note or photo?

The assistant reads text only. It asks the customer to type what they need and offers a follow-up from your team.

Setup and your number

Does it need a new phone number?

Yes. WhatsApp's business platform needs a number that isn't already used on regular WhatsApp. We'll walk you through it.

Is there a time limit on replies?

WhatsApp allows free-form replies for 24 hours after a customer's message, which covers normal customer chats.

How long does setup take?

Most of the work is you sending us your hours, services and booking rules. Connecting a number can take longer if WhatsApp needs to review something, so we'll tell you what to expect when we start.

Control and safety

What if it doesn't know the answer?

It says so and passes the conversation to you instead of guessing.

Will it make up prices or promises?

It is told to use only the information you give it, and never to quote a price you haven't provided. It's AI, so it can still make mistakes. That's why a person confirms anything important, and why we read the real chats with you during the pilot.

Does it confirm bookings?

No. It collects the name, service, day and time, then tells the customer your team will confirm. Your team makes the final call.

Can a customer talk to a real person?

Yes. If they ask, the assistant tells them a team member will follow up, and you get an alert.

Can I still reply myself?

Yes. Anything the assistant can't settle is flagged to you right away, so you can follow up with the customer yourself whenever you like.

Can I see the conversations?

Every conversation is logged, and we can send you a copy on request.

Privacy and cost

Who can see my customers' messages?

Messages pass through WhatsApp, our automation platform and an AI service so replies can be written. We use them only to run your assistant, and we never sell or share them for anything else. The full details are on the privacy page.

What does it cost?

The pilot is free for 14 days. After that, pricing depends on your business, and we'll agree a simple monthly price with you before anything is charged.

Is there a contract?

No contract for the pilot. You can stop whenever you like.
