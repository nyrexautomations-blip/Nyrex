What customers ask salons on WhatsApp.

The questions a salon or spa gets again and again, and how to have your answers ready.

All guides

If you run a salon or spa, most WhatsApp messages are variations of the same few questions. Knowing them lets you answer faster yourself, or set up an assistant to do it.

The usual questions.

  • Do you do this service? Hair colour, facials, bridal makeup, threading. Keep a clear list of what you offer.
  • What are your timings? Include your closed day and any festival closures.
  • Can I come tomorrow evening? Decide whether you take walk-ins or only appointments.
  • Where are you? A short address and a landmark helps more than a map pin alone.
  • How much is it? Decide which prices you share in chat, and which you confirm after seeing the customer.

Write the answers once.

Put the answers in one note on your phone, with services, hours, address and booking rules. You can paste from it, and it is exactly what an assistant needs to be set up. The guide to writing your business details shows a template.

Decide who confirms bookings.

Customers are happier when they know what happens next. Say who confirms, and roughly when: "Our team will confirm shortly." If you never promise a time you cannot keep, you never let anyone down.
