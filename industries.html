Made for businesses that live on WhatsApp.

The assistant works the same way everywhere: it learns your hours, services and rules. Here's what that looks like in five kinds of business. The conversations are examples with made-up businesses.

Salons and spas

Customers ask about services and book appointments at all hours, usually while you're busy with someone else. The assistant answers from your service list and hours, then collects what your team needs to confirm the slot.

What you tell us

  • Services you offer
  • Opening hours and closed days
  • Whether you share prices, or confirm them yourself
  • Who confirms bookings

Clinics and dentists

Patients mostly ask when you're open, whether a doctor is available and how to get there. The assistant answers those and collects appointment requests. Medical questions are different: you tell us what it must never answer, and it passes them to your team.

What you tell us

  • Opening hours and closed days
  • Which doctors or services to mention
  • Address and directions
  • Topics it must hand to your team
S
Smile Dentalreplies in seconds

Yes, Tuesday evening
Tuesday evening is within our hours. May I have your name and a phone number?

Tuition and coaching

Parents ask about batches, timings and demo classes, often late in the evening. The assistant answers from your batch list and takes demo class requests for you to follow up.

What you tell us

  • Batches, subjects and timings
  • Fee details you're happy to share
  • How demo classes work
  • Who follows up with parents

Restaurants and cafes

The same questions arrive every day: are you open, do you deliver, can I get a table. The assistant answers from your hours and menu details, and passes table requests to your team to confirm.

What you tell us

  • Opening hours, including lunch and dinner times
  • Menu details and takeaway or delivery rules
  • How table requests work
  • Who confirms them

Repair shops

Customers want to know if you can fix their problem and when to bring it in. The assistant answers that, gathers the details of the job, and leaves pricing to your team unless you've given it a price list.

What you tell us

  • What you repair
  • Drop-off hours
  • Whether you quote prices or confirm after inspection
  • What details to collect about each job
