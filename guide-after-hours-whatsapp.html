Handling WhatsApp messages after closing time.

Customers message when they are free, which is often when you are shut. Here is a simple way to make sure they still get a useful reply.

All guides

A customer who messages at 10 pm is often deciding where to go tomorrow. If nobody answers, they may simply message the next business on their list. You do not need to stay awake. You need a plan.

1. Put your hours where customers look.

Add your opening hours and address to your WhatsApp business profile and your Google listing. Many questions are really "are you open?" and the profile can answer that before you do.

2. Make your away message do real work.

"We are closed" is the weakest version. A better one gives hours, location and the one thing people ask most, then says when you will reply. For example:

"Thank you for messaging Om Sai Physio. We are open Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 8 pm, at Station Road, Vasai West. Sessions start at Rs 500. Send your name and preferred time and our team will reply by 10 am tomorrow."

That example is made up. Replace every detail with your own, and only include prices you are happy to share.

3. Write your top five answers once.

Timings, prices, location, how to book, current offers. Keep them in one note so you can paste them in seconds and give the same answer every time.

4. Decide what cannot wait.

Complaints, urgent cases and anything about money should reach you quickly. Everything else can wait for the morning. Write the list down, so whoever checks the phone first knows.

5. Reply to the oldest message first.

In the morning, start with the message that has waited longest. A fast reply the next day still beats no reply.

Where an assistant can help.

A fixed away message cannot answer "do you do this on Sunday, and how much?". An assistant can read the question and answer from the details you gave it, collect the customer's name and preferred time, and alert you. Your team still confirms the booking.
