Where your customers' messages go.

A plain explanation of who sees a message, what each service does with it, and what you can ask us to do.

What happens to one message.

  1. A customer messages your number

    WhatsApp, run by Meta, delivers it. Customers install nothing.

  2. The assistant writes a reply

    It runs on Cloudflare and uses only the information you gave us. An AI service, Google Gemini, writes the reply text.

  3. Tricky ones come to you

    If a person is needed, an email alert with the customer's name, number and message is sent to you.

Who handles what.

The services a message passes through
ServiceWhat it doesWhat it receives
WhatsApp (Meta)Delivers messages between your customer and the assistant.The messages and the phone numbers.
CloudflareRuns the assistant and stores the conversation records.Messages, replies, times and the customer's profile name.
Google GeminiWrites the reply text.The recent conversation, the new message and the profile name.
Gmail and BrevoSend you the hand-off alert emails.The alert: name, number and message.

Each service handles data under its own terms. We do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

What we do not do.

  • We do not sell customer information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not ask the assistant to collect payment card numbers, passwords or medical details, and customers are asked not to send them.
  • We do not let the assistant guess. It is told to use only what you provided, and a person confirms anything important, such as a booking.

What you can ask for.

  • What we hold about your business and your customers' conversations.
  • Deletion of the conversation records. Records are not deleted automatically yet, so email us and we will remove them.
  • To stop the pilot at any time, with no contract to cancel.

The pilot agreement says in plain words who owns the data and what happens when the pilot ends. The full privacy policy covers the customer side.
