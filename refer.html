Know a shop that needs this? Get Rs 300.

If you know a local business that gets WhatsApp messages, send them our way. When they become a paying customer, we thank you in cash.

How it works.

  1. You tell the shop about Nyrex. Or send them this site. They can chat with a live demo on their own details at nyrex.pages.dev/trial.html.
  2. They tell us your name. They can mention it when they message us, or you can message us the shop's name and area.
  3. They try the free 14-day pilot. No card, no contract. If it is not useful, they stop and owe nothing.
  4. They pay for their first month. That is Rs 999 for Starter or Rs 1,999 for Growth.
  5. You get Rs 300 on UPI, within a few days of their first payment.

If you are already a Nyrex customer.

Instead of cash, you get one free month for each shop you send who becomes a paying customer.

The small print.

  • Only after they pay. No reward for a free pilot that does not continue.
  • New shops only. A shop we were already in touch with before you sent them does not count.
  • One reward per shop, paid once.
  • Your name is not shared with the shop beyond what you want to tell them.

Who is a good shop to send?

Any business where customers message first: clinics, classes, salons, photographers, caterers, event planners, repair shops. If the owner says "I can't always reply in time", it fits. See who it is for.
