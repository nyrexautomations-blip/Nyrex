What the assistant handles

  • Opening hoursAnswered from the hours you give us
  • ServicesDescribed the way you describe them
  • PricesShared only if you give them to us
  • Booking requestsCollects name, service, day and time for your team to confirm
  • LanguagesReplies in the language the customer writes in
  • ComplaintsApologises and flags you right away
  • Anything it can't answerPassed to you instead of guessed

Stays inside your rules.

You decide what it can say. It doesn't improvise about things you haven't told it.

  • It only uses what you tell it.Hours, services, prices and booking rules come from you.
  • It never confirms a booking itself.It collects the details and your team confirms the slot.
  • It never makes up prices or promises.If it doesn't know, it says so and flags you.
  • Customers can ask for a person.The assistant tells them a team member will follow up, and you get an alert.
  • It says it's automated if asked.No pretending to be a person.

Live in three steps.

  1. You tell us the basics

    Hours, services, how booking works, and what the assistant should never promise. A short form or a WhatsApp chat is enough.

  2. We connect your number

    Your business WhatsApp number gets the assistant. Customers keep messaging you the way they already do.

  3. It answers, you stay in control

    Customers get instant replies in their own language. Anything that needs you is flagged right away.
