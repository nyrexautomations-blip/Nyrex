What the assistant handles

Everything a front desk answers on WhatsApp, using only what you tell it.

  • Opening hours

    Answered from the hours you give us, including the days you're closed.

  • Services

    Described the way you describe them, in your tone.

  • Prices

    Shared only if you give them to us. Otherwise it says your team will confirm.

  • Booking requests

    Collects name, service, day and time. Your team confirms the slot.

  • Languages

    Replies in the language the customer writes in. We test the ones your customers use.

  • Remembers the chat

    Doesn't ask again for a name or time the customer already gave.

  • Complaints

    Apologises, says a person will be in touch, and flags you right away.

  • Hand-offs

    Anything it can't answer goes to you by email, with the customer's name, number and message.

Stays inside your rules.

You decide what it can say. It doesn't improvise about things you haven't told it.

How it stays safe
  • It only uses what you tell it.Hours, services, prices and booking rules come from you.
  • It never confirms a booking itself.It collects the details and your team confirms the slot.
  • It never makes up prices or promises.If it doesn't know, it says so and flags you.
  • Customers can ask for a person.The assistant tells them a team member will follow up, and you get an alert.
  • It says it's automated if asked.No pretending to be a person.

Live in three steps.

  1. You tell us the basics

    Hours, services, how booking works, and what the assistant should never promise. A short form or a WhatsApp chat is enough.

  2. We connect your number

    Your business WhatsApp number gets the assistant. Customers keep messaging you the way they already do.

  3. It answers, you stay in control

    Customers get instant replies in their own language. Anything that needs you is flagged right away.

Read every answer
Do my customers need to install anything?

No. They message your WhatsApp number as usual.

Does it confirm bookings?

No. It collects the name, service, day and time, then tells the customer your team will confirm. Your team makes the final call.

Will it make up prices or promises?

It is told to use only the information you give it, and never to quote a price you haven't provided. It's AI, so it can still make mistakes. That's why a person confirms anything important, and why we read the real chats with you during the pilot.

Does it need a new phone number?

Yes. WhatsApp's business platform needs a number that isn't already used on regular WhatsApp. We'll walk you through it.

What does it cost?

The pilot is free for 14 days. After that, pricing depends on your business, and we'll agree a simple monthly price with you before anything is charged.
