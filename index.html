What the assistant handles

Common questions

Opening hours, services, location and anything you've told it about your business.

Booking requests

Collects name, service, day and time, then passes the summary to your team to confirm.

Any language

Replies in the language the customer writes in, in short, friendly messages.

Hand-off to you

Anything it isn't sure about goes to you instead of a guess. It never makes up prices or promises.

Questions you might have

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. They message your WhatsApp number as usual.

What if it doesn't know the answer?

It says so and passes the conversation to you instead of guessing.

Can I still reply myself?

Yes. You can read every chat and take over whenever you like.

Does it need a new phone number?

Yes. WhatsApp's business platform needs a number that isn't already used on regular WhatsApp. We'll walk you through it.

Is there a time limit on replies?

WhatsApp allows free-form replies for 24 hours after a customer's message, which covers normal customer chats.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on your business. Message us and we'll tell you before anything is set up.
