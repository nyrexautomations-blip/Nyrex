This is a short, plain-language agreement between Nyrex ("we") and the business named below ("you") for a free trial of our WhatsApp assistant. Nothing here is hidden in small print.

1. What we will do

  • Set up an assistant that replies to your customers' WhatsApp messages using the hours, services, prices and rules you give us.
  • Send you an email alert, with the customer's name, number and message, when the assistant hands a conversation to you.
  • Read real conversations with you during the pilot and fix what is wrong.

2. The pilot

  • The pilot lasts 14 days from the start date above and is free. There is no contract and nothing is charged.
  • When the 14 days end, the assistant stops unless we both agree to continue. If we agree to continue, we will agree a simple monthly price with you in writing before anything is charged.

3. Stopping

  • Either of us can stop the pilot at any time, for any reason, by sending a message to the other. We will switch the assistant off promptly, and no later than the next working day.
  • No notice period and no cancellation fee.

4. What the assistant will and will not do

  • It uses only the information you give us. It does not confirm bookings, and it does not quote a price you have not provided. Your team makes the final call.
  • It says it is automated if a customer asks, and passes the customer to you if they ask for a person.
  • It is AI, so it can still make mistakes. We do not promise that every reply will be correct, that it will always be available, or any particular number of bookings or customers. You stay responsible for confirming anything important with your customers.

5. Your information and your customers' information

  • Your business information (hours, services, prices, rules) stays yours. We use it only to run the assistant for you.
  • Customer conversations are your customers' messages to your business. We use them only to run the assistant, check what was said and fix problems. We do not sell them or use them for advertising.
  • To work, messages pass through WhatsApp (Meta), Cloudflare, an AI service (Google Gemini) and email services (Gmail and Brevo), each under its own terms. Our privacy policy describes this in full.
  • When the pilot ends, or whenever you ask, we will delete your business information and your customers' conversation records within 7 days and confirm it to you in writing, unless the law requires us to keep something.
  • You confirm that you are allowed to use the WhatsApp number named above for your business, and that you will follow WhatsApp's business rules.

6. Fair use and limits

  • Use the assistant for your own business and its genuine customers only.
  • The pilot is provided as it is. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for lost sales or indirect losses, and our total responsibility to you is limited to what you paid us, which for the free pilot is nothing. This does not limit anything the law does not allow us to limit.

7. General

  • This agreement is governed by the laws of India. If we disagree, we will first try to settle it by talking.
  • Changes to this agreement must be agreed in writing, and a message from both of us counts as writing.
