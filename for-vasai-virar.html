Who it is for here.

Any local business that gets the same WhatsApp questions again and again. Customers can write in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

  • Salons and spas
  • Clinics, dentists and physiotherapists
  • Tuition and coaching classes
  • Cafes and bakeries
  • Repair shops
  • Gyms, studios and any other business

The free pilot.

Nyrex is new, which is why the first 14 days are free. You see it working on your own business before you decide anything.

  • Free for 14 days, no card, no contract
  • You tell us your hours, services and rules in about 30 minutes
  • Your team confirms bookings, the assistant never does
  • After the pilot: Starter Rs 999 a month or Growth Rs 1,999 a month, paid by UPI

Where we work.

Vasai West (Bhabola, Manikpur, Nirmal), Vasai East (Evershine City, Waliv), Virar, Nalasopara and Naigaon. The setup is done online, so businesses anywhere in India can use Nyrex too.

Questions.

Is the pilot really free?

Yes. Free for 14 days, no card, no contract and no setup fee. If you continue, Starter is Rs 999 a month and Growth is Rs 1,999 a month, paid by UPI.

Which languages does it reply in?

English, Hindi and Hinglish.

Who confirms bookings?

Your team does. The assistant collects the details and passes the request to you. It does not confirm bookings or agree custom quotes.

Does Nyrex work outside Vasai and Virar?

Yes. Nyrex is based in Vasai West, but the setup is done online and works for businesses anywhere in India.
