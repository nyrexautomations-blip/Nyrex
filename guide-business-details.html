How to write your business details for a WhatsApp assistant.

A simple template for the information an assistant needs, so it answers like you would.

All guides

An assistant is only as good as the information behind it. A single page of clear details is enough. Copy the template below, fill it in, and keep it somewhere you can find it.

The template.

  • Business: name, what you do, and the area you are in.
  • Hours: each day, plus closed days and holidays.
  • Services or products: one line each.
  • Prices to share: only the ones you are happy to put in a chat.
  • Booking: appointment, walk-in or call. Who confirms, and how quickly.
  • Never say or promise: things like discounts, delivery times or medical advice.
  • Hand to a person: what should go straight to you, such as complaints or custom quotes.

Keep it honest.

Leave out what is not true or not fixed. If a price changes often, do not put it in. It is better for an assistant to say "our team will confirm" than to share an old number.

Check it against real chats.

Look at your last ten customer messages. Could your page answer each one? Whatever it cannot answer is the first thing to add.
