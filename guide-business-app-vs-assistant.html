WhatsApp Business auto-reply or an assistant: what is the difference?

The free WhatsApp Business app already does a lot. Here is what it covers, where it stops, and when an assistant is worth trying.

All guides

If you run a small business, start with what you already have. The WhatsApp Business app is free, and for many shops it is enough.

What the WhatsApp Business app gives you.

  • A business profile with your hours, address and description
  • A greeting message for new customers
  • An away message for when you are not available
  • Quick replies, so you can paste common answers fast
  • Labels to sort chats, and a catalog for products

Where fixed auto-replies stop.

The greeting and away messages are fixed text. They send the same words to everyone and do not read the question. If a customer asks "Are you open Sunday, and how much is a haircut?", they get your standard message, and someone still has to answer the real question.

What an assistant does differently.

An assistant reads each message and answers from the details you gave it: your hours, services and prices. It can ask for the customer's name and preferred time, and pass the request to you. When it does not know something, it should say so and hand over to you instead of guessing.

When the Business app is enough.

If you get only a few messages a day, mostly while you are free, quick replies and an away message will serve you well. Adding more tools would just be extra work.

When an assistant is worth trying.

  • You get ten or more WhatsApp questions a day
  • Most of them are the same few questions
  • Messages arrive at night or while you are with a customer
  • You lose track of who still needs a reply

What Nyrex does not do.

Nyrex does not confirm bookings and does not agree custom quotes. Your team does that. It answers from what you tell it, and passes the rest to you.
