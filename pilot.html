How the 14 days go.

  1. Before you startWe show you a 30-second demo of the assistant answering questions about your business, using the details you send us. You decide whether to go ahead.
  2. Day 1After we confirm your number setup, the assistant goes live. We test it with you first, so you've seen the replies before a customer does.
  3. Day 7We read the real conversations together and fix anything it got wrong.
  4. Day 14You decide: continue, adjust, or stop. If you stop, you owe nothing.

What we need from you.

Most of it you can send in one WhatsApp message or email.

Open the pilot checklist · Hinglish mein padhein

  • Business name, what you do and where you are
  • Opening hours and days you're closed
  • Services, and the prices you want it to share
  • How booking works and who confirms it
  • A WhatsApp number check. Our current setup needs a number not already registered in the regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app. We explain options and any impact before you decide; don't move or disconnect your current number first.
  • An email address for hand-off alerts

After the pilot.

If you continue, plans start at Rs 999 a month, and we agree the plan with you before anything is charged. See pricing.

If you stop, you owe nothing, and we delete your conversation records if you ask.

Meta currently includes 1,000 service messages per WhatsApp Business number each month. After that, Meta charges per delivered service message; other message categories have different rates. Provider or platform fees may also apply. We will confirm the applicable costs for your setup before you decide. See WhatsApp's current pricing FAQ.
