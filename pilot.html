How the 14 days go.

  1. Before you startWe show you a 30-second demo of the assistant answering questions about your business, using the details you send us. You decide whether to go ahead.
  2. Day 1The assistant goes live on your WhatsApp number. We test it with you first, so you've seen the replies before a customer does.
  3. Day 7We read the real conversations together and fix anything it got wrong.
  4. Day 14You decide: continue, adjust, or stop. If you stop, you owe nothing.

What we need from you.

Most of it you can send in one WhatsApp message or email.

  • Business name, what you do and where you are
  • Opening hours and days you're closed
  • Services, and the prices you want it to share
  • How booking works and who confirms it
  • A WhatsApp number that isn't already used in the regular WhatsApp app (we help with this)
  • An email address for hand-off alerts

After the pilot.

If you continue, we agree a simple monthly price with you before anything is charged.

If you stop, you owe nothing, and we delete your conversation records if you ask.

Meta, which runs WhatsApp's business platform, has its own message pricing. In most cases replies to customers who message you first cost little or nothing, and we'll show you the current pricing before you commit.
