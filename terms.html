Terms of Service

Last updated: 8 November 2026

These terms cover this website and the WhatsApp reply service Nyrex sets up for businesses. They are written in plain language. The pilot agreement is the document you agree to before a pilot starts, and it covers the detail of a particular setup. If the two ever differ, the signed or confirmed pilot agreement for your business applies. Questions: nyrexautomations@gmail.com.

What Nyrex does

Nyrex sets up an assistant that answers WhatsApp messages for your business, using the hours, services, prices and rules you give us. It hands bookings and anything it cannot settle to your team. Nyrex does not confirm bookings, agree custom quotes or give professional advice. Your team does.

The free pilot and paid plans

  • The pilot is free for 14 days. It needs no card and no payment. You can stop at any time.
  • If you continue, the Starter plan is Rs 999 a month and the Growth plan is Rs 1,999 a month. The plan and price are confirmed with you in writing before anything is charged.
  • Payment is by UPI, once a month. There is no contract and no setup fee. You can stop at any time and we stop charging from the next month.
  • WhatsApp and other provider fees are separate from the Nyrex price and may apply. We tell you the fees for your setup before you decide. See pricing.
  • Founding businesses keep their price for as long as they stay with us.

What you are responsible for

  • The information you give us (hours, prices, services, rules) is accurate and kept up to date. The assistant answers from that information.
  • You have the right to use the WhatsApp number you give us, and you follow WhatsApp's own business rules.
  • You do not use the assistant for anything unlawful, misleading, or abusive, or to send customers messages they did not ask for.
  • Your team confirms anything important, such as a booking, before relying on it.

Automated replies can be wrong

Replies are written by AI. The assistant is told to use only what you provided, but it can still make mistakes. We work to fix problems quickly when you tell us, and we do not promise that every reply will be correct, or that the service will never be interrupted. It depends on services run by others, such as WhatsApp, which we do not control.

Results

Nyrex helps you answer messages faster. We do not promise any number of extra customers, bookings or sales. The figures on our estimator are your own numbers worked out on the page, not a forecast.

Your data

Your business information and your customers' messages stay yours. How they are handled is explained on the privacy page and on where messages go. To have data removed, use delete my data.

Stopping

You can stop at any time by messaging or emailing us. We can also stop the service if these terms are broken or if WhatsApp stops allowing it, and we will tell you if that happens.

Limits on what we are liable for

To the extent the law allows, Nyrex is not liable for indirect losses, such as lost sales or missed bookings, and for anything else our liability is limited to the amount you paid Nyrex in the month the problem happened. Nothing here limits a right you have under Indian law that cannot be limited.

Changes and law

If we change these terms we update the date above, and a change to price or plan is agreed with you before it applies to you. These terms are governed by the laws of India.
