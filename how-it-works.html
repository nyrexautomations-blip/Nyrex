Set up once. Replies run by themselves.

What happens from your first message to us to your first customer reply, and what you stay in control of.

  1. You tell us the basics

    Hours, services, how booking works, and what the assistant should never promise.

  2. We connect your number

    Your business WhatsApp number gets the assistant, through WhatsApp's business platform.

  3. It answers, you stay in control

    Instant replies for customers. Anything that needs a person is flagged to you.

What we ask you for.

Fifteen minutes of your time. A WhatsApp chat or a short form is enough.

Tick off the pilot checklist

  • Business name and what you do
  • Opening hours and days you're closed
  • Services, and the prices you want it to share
  • How booking works and who confirms it
  • The tone you want: friendly, formal, playful
  • Anything it must never promise or answer
  • Who should get alerts, and where

Paste it into WhatsApp or email, fill in each line, and send it to nyrexautomations@gmail.com or @nyrexai on Instagram.

When it can't answer, you hear about it.

The assistant never guesses. When a customer needs a person, this is what happens.

  1. The customer asksA question, a booking request or a complaint.
  2. The assistant answers what it knowsUsing only the information you gave it.
  3. It tells the customer a person will follow upFor a booking, a complaint, or anything it has no answer for.
  4. You get an alertBy email, with the customer's name, number, message and the reply that was sent.
  5. You follow up yourselfConfirm the slot, fix the problem, or reply personally.

A typical day with it running.

You keep running the shop. The assistant covers the gaps.

  1. OvernightCustomers who message after closing get answers about your hours and services within seconds. Booking requests wait for you, with the details already collected.
  2. MorningYou open your alerts, see who needs a follow-up, and confirm slots with one tap on the WhatsApp link in each email.
  3. MiddayWhile you're with a customer, new chats still get a reply. Nobody waits for you to look at your phone.
  4. EveningAnything unusual, like a complaint or a question it had no answer for, has already been flagged to you.

Good to know about WhatsApp.

  • The assistant needs a number that isn't already in the regular WhatsApp app.That could be a new SIM or a number you're ready to move. We walk you through it.
  • Free-form replies work for 24 hours after a customer's message.That covers normal customer chats.
  • It reads text.If someone sends a voice note or photo, it asks them to type what they need and offers a follow-up from your team.
  • Replies are written by AI.The assistant says so if a customer asks, and your team confirms anything important, like a booking.
