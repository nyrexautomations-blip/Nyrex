A small business for small businesses.

Nyrex is an independent business in India. We set up and run a WhatsApp assistant for local businesses, so customers get an answer even when you cannot reply.

Who it is for.

Any business that gets customer messages on WhatsApp: shops, studios, clinics, classes, restaurants, services. If customers keep asking you the same questions, it fits. See how it works for any business.

What we do.

We build the assistant, connect it to your WhatsApp number, and keep it running. You tell us your hours, services and rules. The assistant answers customers from that, and hands anything it cannot settle to you.

How we work.

  • Plain language. No jargon, no hidden terms. The pilot agreement is one short page.
  • No contract. The Nyrex service is free for 14 days, and you can stop at any time. Separate WhatsApp or provider fees may apply; we confirm them before setup.
  • You stay in control. The assistant does not guess prices or promises. A person confirms anything important.
  • You deal with the people who set it up. If something is wrong, tell us and we fix it.

What we are not.

Nyrex is not part of WhatsApp or Meta, and is not endorsed by them. We use WhatsApp's business platform, like many other businesses do. Replies are written by AI, which can make mistakes, so we test with you during the pilot and read real conversations together.

Start small.

Looking for one local business to pilot with. We show a 30-second demo on your own details first, then set it up with you. If it is not useful, you stop, and you owe nothing.
