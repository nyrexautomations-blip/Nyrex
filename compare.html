Nyrex fits when.

The same questions keep coming and you cannot always answer fast.

  • Customers ask about hours, services and availability, again and again
  • You miss messages while you are working or after closing
  • Bookings need a few details collected before you confirm
  • You want to stay in control of prices and promises

When it may not be right.

  • Very few messages. If you get a handful a week, replying yourself is simpler.
  • Custom quotes and negotiation. The assistant passes these to you. It does not agree prices.
  • Customers who only phone. Nyrex works on WhatsApp messages, not calls.
  • Urgent or medical advice. The assistant does not give it. It hands the chat to a person.
